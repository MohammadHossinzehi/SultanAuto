What To Do Next

A few simple steps at the scene make the repair, and any claim, much easier.

Make it safe

Stop safely, put your hazards on and check everyone is OK. Call 999 if anyone is hurt.

Take photos

Photograph the damage, the cars and the scene from a few angles.

Swap details

Get the other driver's name, number, registration and insurance details.

Send them to us

WhatsApp your photos to us and we'll tell you what the repair needs.
