Our story

Built on Word of Mouth

Sultan Autos is run by Dawud and his team from our workshop at 25 Athole Street, Birmingham.

We've grown the way a good local garage should: by doing honest work at a fair price, and letting our customers do the talking. Some have been with us for years. Many found us because a friend told them about us.

Every car gets the same treatment, whether it's a scuffed bumper or a Tesla that needs serious bodywork. We keep you updated with photos and videos while we work, and we don't hand a car back until we're happy with it.
