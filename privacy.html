Privacy Policy

How Sultan Autos uses the details you give us when you ask for a quote or book a repair.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

Sultan Autos Ltd is a car body repair and paint shop at 25 Athole Street, Birmingham B12 0DA. We decide how the personal information described here is used, which makes us the "controller" under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, call or WhatsApp us on 07599 999112, or write to us at the address above.

What we collect

  • Quote form: your name, phone number, car make and model, registration (optional), the service you need and any description of the damage.
  • WhatsApp and phone calls: your phone number, your name, your messages and any photos or videos of your car that you send us.
  • If we repair your car: the details we need to carry out the work, keep you updated and invoice you.

How the quote form works

The form on our contact page does not save your details on this website or send them to a database. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing what you typed. Your details are passed to WhatsApp to fill in that message, and they reach us when you press send. You can change or delete the message before sending it.

WhatsApp is owned by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote, because you asked us to (steps before entering into a contract).
  • To carry out your repair and keep you updated with photos and videos (performing our contract with you).
  • To keep business and tax records (a legal obligation).

We will never sell your information.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to:

  • with the services we use to talk to you, such as WhatsApp and our phone provider;
  • with others involved in your repair, such as your insurer, when you ask us to;
  • with our accountant, HMRC or others when the law requires it.

Cookies and this website

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts and images are hosted on our own site. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.

The map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps. When it loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address and may set cookies, under Google's privacy policy. Links to Google Maps and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. If we repair your car, we keep job and invoice records for as long as the law requires for tax purposes, usually six years.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to us using it;
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere.

Some of these rights only apply in certain situations. To make a request, call or WhatsApp us on 07599 999112. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
