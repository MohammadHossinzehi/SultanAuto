Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I get a quote?
The quickest way is to send a few photos of the damage on WhatsApp to 07599 999112. You can also call us or visit the shop on Athole Street.
How long will my repair take?
It depends on the damage. Small jobs can be quick, bigger repairs take longer. We'll give you an idea of timing when we quote.
Will the new paint match my car?
Yes. We match the paint to your car's colour and blend it into the panels around the repair so it doesn't stand out.
Do I need to book?
Yes, please get in touch first so we can look at the damage and book you in at a time that suits you.
Will I know how the job is going?
Yes. We send photo and video updates while your car is with us, so you always know where things are.
